Andrew Huck

Andrew Huck is the founder and CEO of Gulfstream Capital, a registered investment advisory firm created to bring sophisticated asset management services to family offices, institutions, high net worth individuals and athletes. Mr. Huck is a Phi Beta Kappa graduate, with highest honors, from Wake Forest University (1996) and subsequently completed his MBA, with a concentration in portfolio theory, from The University of Virginia (2006). Mr. Huck believes that the holy grail of investing consists of a truly diversified portfolio of non-correlated assets, which Gulfstream Capital offers to its wealth management clients through a variety of innovative solutions including farmland, natural resources, public and private equity, venture capital, disruptive technologies, the life sciences, event driven hedge funds, and a variety of strategic real estate investments. Mr. Huck has served on the boards of several local and national philanthropic organizations including, Young Life, The New Canaan Society, FCA, 4 Market Square, The American Heart Association, and Telemachus (a mentoring initiative). In his free time, Mr. Huck is an avid outdoor enthusiast and enjoys golf, tennis, snow skiing, padel, and pickleball. He is married to his college sweetheart, and finds his flow coaching his four children on the athletic fields and in life.
